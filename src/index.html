huff

v2.0 · beta · 2025

Real-time datamosh & glitch-art engine. Load a video or connect a camera, then sculpt temporal tile displacement, feedback loops, flow warps, symmetry, and solarise effects — streamed live to a fullscreen mirror window over WebSocket.

Built with Tauri v1 + p5.js.
Embedded WS relay on port 8787.
