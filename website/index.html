What huff does

Video as raw material

huff treats video the way a sculptor treats clay. Load a file, plug in a camera — then start destroying it. Datamosh breaks the image apart into tiles and scatters them across time. Feedback sends the output back into the input until the image dissolves into itself.

Every parameter is live. Every effect is stackable. MIDI controllers, OSC from a phone, or just a mouse — huff responds in real time without dropped frames, without render queues, without waiting.

The output window is always on. Move it to a second monitor, plug it into a projector, feed it into Resolume or TouchDesigner via Syphon or Spout. What happens on screen happens everywhere.

The toolkit

Eight ways to
break an image

01
Glitch

Samples tiles from past frames and scatters them across the canvas. The core datamosh engine — the bigger the tile, the more raw and blocky the artefact.

Temporal
02
Feedback

The output feeds back into the input with a zoom, translation, and rotation applied each frame. Sustained feedback creates hypnotic tunnels and infinite mirrors.

Spatial
03
Trails

Blends stacked ghost frames underneath the current output at decreasing opacity. With luma keying, only bright elements trail — dark areas vanish cleanly.

Temporal
04
Scanlines

Horizontal bands drift across the frame, each displacing their slice from a different point in history. Creates broadcast glitch, VHS dropout, and signal corruption aesthetics.

Spatial · Temporal
05
Flow Warp

A noise field distorts the UV coordinates of every pixel, creating organic liquid displacement. The Implode mode pulls everything toward the centre like a gravity well.

Spatial
06
Symmetry

Mirrors the frame about one or both axes at an adjustable position. In combination with feedback, it creates kaleidoscopic recursive patterns.

Spatial
07
Solarize

Inverts pixels above a luminance threshold, channelled independently through R/G/B multipliers. Creates chemical darkroom aesthetics and psychedelic colour inversions.

Colour
08
Clusters

When enabled, glitch tiles concentrate into radial zones with their own physics — speed, inertia, drift. Glitch centres orbit and collide like living organisms.

Spatial · Physics
Who it's for

Made for the stage,
the screen, the wall

Live VJ sets

MIDI-map every parameter to a physical controller. Syphon or Spout your output directly into Resolume, VDMX, or MadMapper — no OBS, no capture card, no lag.

Experimental film

Process found footage in real time. Record the canvas window with OBS. Build sequences that couldn't exist in After Effects — because they evolve differently every take.

Installation art

Set a preset and let it run. Feedback and cluster physics create hours of non-repeating evolution from a single source loop. No timeline, no automation required.

Live streaming

Feed the output into OBS via window capture. Apply effects in response to chat via OSC from a Max/MSP or TouchDesigner patch. Make the stream interactive.
